# Ski road-trip planner

Unselected concept by Rylan Wade. A resort-data worker checks official terrain, pass, and operating facts; a review worker assesses dated skier experiences; an itinerary worker selects resorts and allocates ski days under the group's constraints. A supervisor handles follow-up. Plans show source dates, pass/terrain limits, tradeoffs, and unresolved weather or travel assumptions. [Selected project](../../project.md).

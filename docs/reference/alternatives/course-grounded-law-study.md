# Course-grounded law study

Unselected concept by Rylan Wade. A course-material worker interprets assigned readings, a recent-case worker checks relevant decisions and jurisdiction, and an outline worker builds a cited topic outline. A supervisor coordinates bounded follow-up. Each rule, exception, and comparison needs an inspectable passage; unsupported or conflicting material stays visible. Start with one course and jurisdiction, then review outputs with a law student or instructor. [Selected project](../../project.md).
